Where things stand

Filming wrapped at Ardross Castle in June 2026, the full 21-strong cast is confirmed, and the BBC dropped its first look trailer on 27 August 2026. The BBC still hasn't made an official announcement, but the date is effectively out: at a live recording of The Rest Is Entertainment podcast at London's Southbank Centre, Richard Osman let slip on stage that the series starts 23 September 2026 — earlier than series one's 8 October 2025 launch — before catching himself and noting it was "absolutely embargoed." Odds below are from William Hill as of late May 2026 and will move once the show actually airs.

How the game works

A small group of celebrities are secretly appointed as Traitors. Everyone else is a Faithful. Each day, the group completes missions to build a prize pot. Each night the Traitors secretly "murder" a Faithful. At daily Round Tables, everyone votes to banish who they believe is a Traitor. If all Traitors are banished, the Faithfuls share the prize — but if a Traitor reaches the final, they take it all for their charity.
